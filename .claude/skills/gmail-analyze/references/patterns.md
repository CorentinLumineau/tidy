# Common Email Patterns Reference

## Sender Domain Categories

### Newsletters
Domains typically containing:
- `substack.com`
- `mailchimp.com`
- `newsletter`
- `digest`
- `weekly`
- `daily`
- `update`
- `bulletin`

### Notifications
Domains typically containing:
- `github.com`
- `gitlab.com`
- `bitbucket.org`
- `jira`
- `slack.com`
- `notion.so`
- `linear.app`
- `asana.com`
- `trello.com`

### Shopping & Orders
Domains typically containing:
- `amazon`
- `ebay`
- `aliexpress`
- `shopify`
- `order`
- `shipping`
- `tracking`
- `receipt`
- `invoice`

### Social Media
Domains typically containing:
- `facebook.com`
- `facebookmail.com`
- `twitter.com`
- `x.com`
- `linkedin.com`
- `instagram.com`
- `tiktok.com`

### Automated/System
Indicators:
- `noreply@`
- `no-reply@`
- `donotreply@`
- `notifications@`
- `alerts@`
- `system@`

### Financial
Domains typically containing:
- Bank names
- `paypal.com`
- `stripe.com`
- `venmo.com`
- `statement`
- `alert`

## Confidence Levels

| Level | Criteria |
|-------|----------|
| High | 10+ emails, clear domain pattern |
| Medium | 5-9 emails, recognizable pattern |
| Low | 3-4 emails, uncertain category |

## Pareto Analysis

Apply 80/20 rule:
- Focus on top 20% of senders (by volume)
- These typically generate 80% of inbox noise
- Prioritize filters for highest-impact domains

## Gmail Search Syntax

Useful queries for analysis:
- `newer_than:30d` - Last 30 days
- `from:@domain.com` - From specific domain
- `is:unread` - Unread only
- `has:attachment` - With attachments
- `larger:5M` - Larger than 5MB
- `category:promotions` - Gmail's auto-category
